About

Customer service habits, applied to business operations.

I'm a remote support professional based in the Philippines. My background is customer service, and over the last two years it has grown into operations work for U.S.-based businesses: keeping CRM records accurate, following up with leads, setting appointments, and collecting the documents a funding application needs.

Before that I handled claims, benefits, and rebate questions on BPO accounts. That work taught me to follow a procedure, document every interaction, and stay patient with people who just want a clear answer. I have also spent time cold calling property owners and booking appointments for local real estate representatives.

What I do best is the follow-through. I update the record, send the follow-up, and flag what needs a decision. I work independently in GoHighLevel, Slack, Notion, and Outlook, and I know when to bring something to the owner instead of guessing.

Scope, stated plainly

I'm a CRM user and operations support, not a developer or automation architect. I don't give financial, legal, or credit advice, and I'm not a licensed medical professional.
